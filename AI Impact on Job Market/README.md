 AI Job Market Trends Dashboard – Tableau

📊 Project Overview
The AI Job Market Trends Dashboard is an interactive Tableau data visualization project designed to analyze job market trends, salary patterns, automation risks, and the impact of Artificial Intelligence across different industries.

The dashboard provides insights into employment opportunities, remote work trends, salary distributions, experience requirements, and gender diversity to help understand how AI is influencing the global job market.

 🛠️ Tools Used
- Tableau – Data Visualization and Interactive Dashboard Development
- Excel – Data Exploration and Analysis

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Average Remote Work Ratio: 49.84%
- Average Automation Risk: 50.15%
- Average Salary: $90,120
- Job Openings (2024)
- Projected Job Openings (2030)

📊 Dashboard Features
1. Remote Work by Industry – Analyzes remote work opportunities across different industries.
2. Salary by Industry – Compares average salaries across various industry sectors.
3. AI Impact Across Industries – Visualizes the impact of AI on job opportunities across industries.
4. Experience vs Salary – Examines the relationship between required experience and average salary.
5. Job Openings by Location – Displays the geographical distribution of job opportunities.
6. Automation Risk vs Salary – Analyzes the relationship between automation risk and median salary.
7. Gender Diversity by Industry – Compares gender diversity percentages across industries.
8. Required Education – Shows the educational qualifications required for different job opportunities.

 🎯 Project Objectives
- Understand global job market trends.
- Identify industries with higher salary opportunities.
- Analyze the relationship between AI automation risk and salaries.
- Explore remote work opportunities across industries.
- Understand the influence of experience and education on employment.
- Identify geographical differences in job opportunities.

💡 Key Learnings
- Hands-on experience with Tableau dashboard development.
- Data visualization and analytical storytelling.
- Working with multiple chart types and geographical maps.
- Understanding relationships between business metrics.
- Presenting complex data through an interactive dashboard.

 📌 Conclusion
This project provides a comprehensive overview of AI-driven job market trends and helps visualize how automation, industry, experience, education, and remote work are connected to employment opportunities and salary patterns.
